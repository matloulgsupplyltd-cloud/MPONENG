Mining operations

Open-cast and underground mining, planned for safety and recovery

Open-cast mining

Open-cast operations use drilling, blasting, loading and hauling in carefully designed benches. Mine plans balance recovery, stability, water management and the final landform.

  • Bench design set by geotechnical data
  • Fleet dispatch to cut idle time and fuel use
  • Progressive rehabilitation behind the working face

Underground mining

Underground operations reach deeper orebodies with a smaller surface footprint. Ground support, ventilation and escape routes are designed in from the first plan.

  • Ground control and ventilation engineered per section
  • Mechanised, remote-ready equipment where practical
  • Strict access control and emergency preparedness

Mineral resources

What we produce

Manganese ore

Lump and fines from Tsholo Ridge, used in steelmaking and ferromanganese production. Sold to steelmakers and alloy producers.

Chromite ore

Metallurgical and chemical grade chrome from Marula Deep, supplied to ferrochrome smelters.

Upgraded concentrates

Beneficiated products from Sefako Plant, blended to customer specification for consistent quality.

Formal resource and reserve statements are published in our annual reporting, available in the Investors section.

Processing facilities

From run-of-mine ore to saleable product

Our processing plants crush, screen and beneficiate ore to the quality our customers specify. Plant control systems track throughput and quality continuously, so problems are caught early.

  • Crushing and screening circuits
  • Beneficiation and separation
  • Laboratory quality control and sampling
  • Product stockpiling and load-out
